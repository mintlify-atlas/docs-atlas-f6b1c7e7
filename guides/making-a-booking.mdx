Reserving a room in ViniHotel takes you through four pages: hotel detail, a booking dialog, the payment page, and the confirmation screen. The entire flow is covered in this guide from prerequisites through what happens after you confirm.

## Prerequisites

<Warning>
  You must be logged in before you can view hotel details or make a reservation. Unauthenticated users are redirected to `/login`. If you do not have an account, register at `/register` first — new accounts are automatically signed in after registration.
</Warning>

## Booking flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse to a hotel">
    Go to the home page at `/` and find a hotel. Use the search box, filters, or sort order to narrow the list. Click **Ver detalles** or the hotel image to open the hotel detail page.

    For a full walkthrough of the listing and filtering features, see [Browse and search hotels](/guides/browsing-hotels).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a room">
    On the hotel detail page (`/hotel/:id`), scroll to **Habitaciones Disponibles**. Each room card shows the `type`, `capacity`, `price` per night, and available unit count. Click **Reservar Ahora** on the room you want. Rooms with `available === 0` or a status of `cleaning`, `maintenance`, or `unavailable` cannot be selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose check-in and check-out dates">
    A dialog opens for that room. Select your **Fecha de entrada** (`checkIn`) and **Fecha de salida** (`checkOut`). The check-out date must be after the check-in date. Both dates are required — the confirm button stays disabled until valid dates are provided.

    Once both dates are set, the dialog shows a live cost preview:

    ```text
    N noche(s) × $price = $totalPrice
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Specify the number of guests">
    Enter the number of guests in the **Número de huéspedes** field. The value must be between 1 and the room's `capacity`. Exceeding the capacity blocks the booking with an alert.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm guest details">
    Review or update the **Nombre completo** (`guestName`) and **Email** (`guestEmail`) fields. These are pre-filled from your logged-in user profile. All four fields — dates, guests, name, and email — must be filled before **Confirmar Reserva** becomes active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a payment method and review the summary">
    After clicking **Confirmar Reserva**, you are taken to the payment page (`/payment`). The left panel shows a booking summary:

    | Field | Value |
    |---|---|
    | Hotel & room | `hotelName` / `roomName` |
    | Check-in | `checkIn` |
    | Check-out | `checkOut` |
    | Guests | `guests` |
    | Subtotal | `totalPrice` |
    | Taxes (16%) | `totalPrice × 0.16` |
    | **Total** | `totalPrice × 1.16` |

    Choose a payment method:

    - **credit_card** — credit card payment
    - **debit-card** — debit card; payment status is automatically set to `paid`
    - **paypal** — PayPal payment
    - **twallet** — T-Wallet digital payment

    Accept the terms and conditions checkbox, then click **Confirmar y Pagar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive booking confirmation">
    A successful payment sends you to `/booking-confirmation`. The page displays a confirmation number and all booking details: hotel, room, dates, guest count, guest name, email, and total paid. Confirmation details are also emailed to `guestEmail`.

    Use the **Ver Mis Reservas** button to navigate to `/my-bookings`, or **Imprimir** to print the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after confirmation

When a booking is created, the `BookingContext` sets the following initial field values:

| Field | Initial value |
|---|---|
| `status` | `confirmed` |
| `paymentStatus` | `paid` (debit card) or `pending` (transfer / QR) |
| `createdAt` | Current ISO timestamp |

### Booking statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `confirmed` | Reservation accepted, awaiting check-in |
| `checked-in` | Guest has checked in; room is `occupied` |
| `checked-out` | Guest has completed their stay; room moves to `cleaning` |
| `cancelled` | Booking was cancelled; payment moves to `refunded` |

### Payment statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `pending` | Payment not yet received (transfer or QR) |
| `paid` | Payment confirmed |
| `refunded` | Payment reversed after cancellation |

## Invoice generation

An invoice is created automatically alongside every booking. The `Invoice` record includes:

```json
{
  "id": "invoice-uuid",
  "bookingId": "booking-uuid",
  "subtotal": 720.00,
  "taxes": 108.00,
  "total": 828.00,
  "additionalCharges": {},
  "paymentMethod": "credit_card",
  "paymentStatus": "pending",
  "issuedDate": "2026-04-29 14:30:00"
}
```

The `additionalCharges` field is a JSON object. It defaults to an empty object `{}` and can contain any extra charge keys such as `resort_fee`, `service_charge`, or `parking`.

You can view and download invoices from the **Facturas** tab on your profile page at `/profile`.
