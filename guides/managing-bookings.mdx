The My Bookings page gives you a consolidated view of every reservation tied to your account — upcoming stays, the current active stay, and your booking history. You can also cancel upcoming reservations directly from this page.

<Info>
  The page at `/my-bookings` requires authentication. If you are not logged in, you will be redirected to `/login`.
</Info>

## Accessing My Bookings

Navigate to `/my-bookings` from the main navigation. The page opens with three summary cards at the top:

- **Total de Reservas** — total number of bookings across all time
- **Próximas** — count of bookings where `checkIn` is in the future
- **Completadas** — count of bookings where `checkOut` is in the past

## Booking sections

Bookings are split into three sections based on their dates relative to today:

### Current stay

If today falls between `checkIn` and `checkOut`, the booking appears in the **Estancia Actual** section with a green "En curso" badge. This section shows:

- `hotelName` and `roomName`
- `checkIn` and `checkOut` dates
- `guests` count
- `guestName` and `guestEmail`
- `totalPrice`

### Upcoming bookings

Reservations where `checkIn` is still in the future appear under **Próximas Reservas** with a blue "Próxima" badge. Each card shows check-in and check-out dates, guest count, and total price. A **Cancelar Reserva** button is available for each upcoming booking.

### Booking history

Past bookings (where `checkOut` has already passed) appear in **Historial de Reservas** with a grey "Completada" badge. These cards are read-only.

## Booking statuses explained

The `status` field on a `Booking` record has five possible values:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="confirmed">
    The reservation has been accepted. The guest has not yet checked in. Bookings created through the standard flow start in this status. Cancellation is allowed while the booking is `confirmed`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="checked-in">
    The guest has checked in. The associated room's `roomStatus` is `occupied`. This status is set by staff via the admin panel and is displayed as "En curso" in the UI.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="checked-out">
    The guest has checked out. The room's status moves to `cleaning`. Displayed as "Completada" in booking history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="cancelled">
    The booking was cancelled. The `paymentStatus` is automatically set to `refunded`. Cancelled bookings cannot be reinstated.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="pending">
    An intermediate status available to admin/staff workflows. Not assigned through the standard customer booking flow.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payment statuses

The `paymentStatus` field tracks the financial state of a booking:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `paid` | Payment was received (set immediately for debit card payments) |
| `pending` | Payment has not yet been confirmed (bank transfer or QR payments start here) |
| `refunded` | Payment was reversed, typically after cancellation |

## How to cancel a booking

You can cancel any booking that still has a future `checkIn` date.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Bookings">
    Navigate to `/my-bookings`. Upcoming reservations appear in the **Próximas Reservas** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Cancel">
    Click **Cancelar Reserva** on the booking you want to cancel. A confirmation prompt appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm cancellation">
    Confirm the action. The booking's `status` is set to `cancelled` and `paymentStatus` is set to `refunded`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Cancellation is irreversible. Once a booking is cancelled, it cannot be reinstated through the application.
</Warning>

## Viewing invoice details

Each completed or confirmed booking has a corresponding invoice. To view the invoice:

1. Go to `/profile` and open the **Facturas** tab.
2. Find the invoice by hotel name, room name, or issue date.
3. Click **Ver Factura** to open a detail dialog showing subtotal, 16% taxes, any additional charges, and the payment method and status.
4. Use **Descargar PDF** to print or save the invoice.

Invoice fields include `subtotal`, `taxes`, `additionalCharges`, `total`, `paymentMethod`, and `paymentStatus`. The `issuedDate` is set to the moment the booking was confirmed.

<Note>
  After cancellation, the invoice's `paymentStatus` is updated to `refunded`. The invoice itself is retained in your invoice history and remains viewable.
</Note>
