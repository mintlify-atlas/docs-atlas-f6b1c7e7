---

The reports page gives administrators a consolidated view of the chain's financial and operational performance. It is accessible at the `/reports` route and derives all metrics in real time from the bookings and invoices data. There are no manual refresh controls — figures update automatically as bookings are created, modified, or cancelled. A single **Exportar Reporte** button is available in the page header for downloading the current report data.

<Warning>
  The reports dashboard is restricted to users with the **admin** role.
</Warning>

## Key performance indicators

Four KPI cards appear at the top of the page, providing an at-a-glance summary of overall performance across all hotels.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Ingresos Totales" icon="dollar-sign">
    The sum of all invoice `total` values (subtotal + taxes). Calculated as `invoices.reduce((sum, inv) => sum + inv.total, 0)`. Displayed in green. Labeled **Todos los tiempos** to indicate it is a cumulative, all-time figure.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Total Reservas" icon="calendar">
    The total count of all bookings in the system regardless of status. Also shows the number of currently **confirmed** active bookings as a sub-label.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tasa de Ocupación" icon="chart-bar">
    Calculated as `(confirmedBookings + completedBookings) / totalBookings * 100`. Expressed as a percentage with one decimal place. The sub-label shows the count of completed bookings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Valor Promedio" icon="users">
    Average revenue per booking: `totalRevenue / totalBookings`. Expressed in USD with two decimal places. Labeled **Por reserva**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Booking status distribution

The **Estado de Reservas** panel shows the current breakdown of all bookings by status, with a count badge and percentage for each category.

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Confirmadas | Bookings with `status: 'confirmed'` — paid and upcoming. |
| Completadas | Bookings with `status: 'completed'` — stay finished, guest checked out. |
| Canceladas | Bookings with `status: 'cancelled'` — reservation was cancelled before or during the stay. |

<Note>
  Bookings in `pending` and `checked-in` status are tracked in the booking management panel but are not separately broken out in the reports status distribution. They are included in the **Total Reservas** count and contribute to the occupancy rate through the `confirmed` + `completed` formula.
</Note>

## Hotel performance ranking

The **Rendimiento por Hotel** panel ranks the top 5 hotels in the chain by booking count. For each hotel it shows:

- Hotel name
- Total number of bookings (`data.count`)
- A proportional bar chart showing that hotel's share of all bookings (`count / totalBookings * 100%`)
- Total revenue generated by the hotel (`data.revenue` in USD)

Hotels are sorted descending by booking count, so the busiest property always appears first. This panel is derived by grouping bookings on `booking.hotelName`.

## Monthly revenue trend

The **Ingresos Mensuales** panel breaks down bookings and revenue by calendar month. Each month row displays:

- Month and year label (e.g. `abril 2026`)
- A badge showing the booking count for that month
- The revenue total for that month in USD
- A gradient progress bar (`green → blue`) proportional to that month's share of total all-time revenue

This section is built by grouping bookings on `new Date(booking.createdAt).toLocaleDateString('es-ES', { month: 'long', year: 'numeric' })`, so months are labeled in Spanish locale.

## Revenue data source

Revenue figures in the reports panel are drawn from the `invoices` table, not directly from `bookings.totalPrice`. Each invoice stores:

- `subtotal` — the booking amount before taxes
- `taxes` — the applied tax or IVA amount
- `total` — the final charged amount (`subtotal + taxes`)

This means the **Ingresos Totales** figure reflects actual invoiced amounts and accounts for any additional charges stored in the `additionalCharges` JSON column (e.g. resort fees, parking, service charges).

```sql
-- Revenue by hotel (underlying query pattern)
SELECT h.nombre, SUM(i.total) as ingresos 
FROM invoices i 
JOIN bookings b ON i.bookingId = b.id 
JOIN rooms r ON b.roomId = r.id 
JOIN hoteles h ON r.hotelId = h.idHotel 
GROUP BY h.idHotel;
```

## Most frequent guests

While the reports UI surfaces per-hotel booking counts, the underlying database supports identifying most frequent guests by booking volume. This query pattern is available for custom reporting:

```sql
SELECT u.nombre, u.apellido, COUNT(b.id) as reservas 
FROM bookings b 
JOIN clientes c ON b.idCliente = c.idCliente 
JOIN usuarios u ON c.idUsuario = u.idUsuario 
GROUP BY u.idUsuario 
ORDER BY reservas DESC 
LIMIT 10;
```
